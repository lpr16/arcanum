# Arcanum

> A cryptography research study in F#, centered on elliptic-curve groups.

Arcanum is a cryptography research study. Its center is the group of points on a short Weierstrass curve over a prime field. The group law is `EllipticCurve.add`, and scalar multiplication is repeated addition. Around that center, the study locks exact constructions a reader can check: modular arithmetic, binary fields, cited checks of existing symmetric code, textbook public-key arithmetic, historical letter ciphers, and one integer lattice.

The project targets F# on .NET 10. It is an educational and research codebase, not a production cryptography library. The work orders are `Docs/antigravity_work_program.md` (phases 0 through 8), `Docs/antigravity_research_program.md` (phases 9 through 48), `Docs/antigravity_long_haul_program.md` (phases 49 through 79), and `Docs/antigravity_depth_program.md` (phases 80 through 128). `Docs/agent_channel.md` records each phase.

## Coordinate field and elliptic-curve group

The mathematical core is implemented in:

- `src/Arcanum.Core/ToyField.fs`: The coordinate field is `ToyField.Residue` at `ToyField.prime` (`17`). Multiplicative inverse is `Modular.modInverse`.
- `src/Arcanum.Core/EllipticCurve.fs`:
  - Curve type is `EllipticCurve.Curve`.
  - `EllipticCurve.isOnCurve` checks the curve equation and requires affine coordinates in `0 .. P - 1`.
  - Group law is `EllipticCurve.add`. Negation is `EllipticCurve.negate`. Doubling is `EllipticCurve.double`.
  - Scalar multiplication is `EllipticCurve.scalarMultiply`, which reduces the scalar modulo `Curve.N`. `EllipticCurve.scalarMultiplyLadder`, `EllipticCurve.naf`, `EllipticCurve.wnaf`, `EllipticCurve.scalarMultiplyNaf`, `EllipticCurve.scalarMultiplyWnaf`, and `EllipticCurve.multiScalarMultiply` (Shamir's trick) are further short-Weierstrass scalar multiplication implementations checked against `add`, with no constant-time claim. `EllipticCurve.multiplyUnreduced` walks a non-negative scalar without reducing it modulo `N`.
  - `EllipticCurve.validatePublicKey` implements SEC 1 section 3.2.2.1: it refuses `Infinity`, refuses any point that fails `isOnCurve`, and refuses any point with `multiplyUnreduced curve curve.N Q <> Infinity`. In a non-cyclic group such as `toy2`, `N * Q = Infinity` only shows the order of `Q` divides `N`; it does not prove `Q` lies in the subgroup generated by `G`.
  - `EllipticCurve.toy` is the curve `y^2 = x^3 + 1` over F_17. It has 18 points. `G = Point(6, 8)` generates them. `N` is `18`. `H` is `1`. `Point(0, 1)` has order `3`. The toy curve is not a secure group.
  - `EllipticCurve.toy2` is the curve `y^2 = x^3 + x` over F_17. It has 16 points and its group is `Z/4 x Z/4` (not cyclic), generated as `a * G + b * Q` by `G = Point(1, 6)` and `Q = Point(3, 8)`. `G = Point(1, 6)` has order 4 and does not generate the group. `N` is `4`, the order of `G`, and every point order divides 4. `H` is `4`. The toy2 curve is not a secure group.
  - `EllipticCurve.toy3` is the curve `y^2 = x^3 + 2x + 2` over F_17. It has prime order 19, so every point other than `Infinity` generates the group. `G = Point(5, 1)`, `N = 19`, and `H = 1`. The toy3 curve is not a secure group.
  - `EllipticCurve.jInvariant` returns `-1728 * (4a)^3 / Delta` modulo `P`, with `Delta = -16 * (4a^3 + 27b^2)`, or `None` when `Delta` is zero. `jInvariant toy` is `Some 0`, `jInvariant toy2` is `Some 11` (`1728 mod 17`), and `jInvariant toy3` is `Some 3`.
  - `EllipticCurve.compress` records `Infinity`, or the x coordinate and the least bit of `y`. `EllipticCurve.decompress` recovers the point with `Modular.modSqrt`, and returns `None` when the square root is missing.
  - `EllipticCurve.countPoints` sums Legendre symbols via `Modular.jacobi` over `0 .. p - 1` on the toy curves (not Schoof's algorithm, and never called on the large curves). `EllipticCurve.twistCoefficients` returns `(a * d^2 mod P, b * d^3 mod P)` for a quadratic non-residue `d` and refuses `d` when `jacobi d P <> -1`.
  - `EllipticCurve.secp256k1` and `EllipticCurve.nistP256` are the large parameter records. Their checks are: the generator is on the curve, unreduced `N * G` is `Infinity`, and unreduced `(N - 1) * G` is `-G`. On `secp256k1`, the cube-root endomorphism `lambda * Q = (beta * x mod p, y)` is checked on `G` and `2G` by ordinary scalar multiplication, without a GLV scalar decomposition. These checks do not take a discrete logarithm or enumerate the group.
- `src/Arcanum.Core/Sec1.fs`: `Sec1.encodePoint` and `Sec1.decodePoint` implement SEC 1 sections 2.3.3 and 2.3.4 octet encoding and decoding (compressed and uncompressed) using `compress` and `decompress`.
- `src/Arcanum.Core/Jacobian.fs`: `Jacobian.JacobianPoint`, `ofAffine`, `toAffine`, `double`, `add`, `addMixed`, `scalarMultiply`, `doubleAMinus3`, and `batchToAffine` implement the short-Weierstrass group law and batch conversion in Jacobian coordinates `(X : Y : Z)` as further implementations checked against `EllipticCurve.add`, with no constant-time claim.
- `src/Arcanum.Core/DiscreteLog.fs`: `DiscreteLog.order`, `bruteForce`, `babyStepGiantStep` (with integer ceiling square root `ceilSqrt`), `pohligHellman`, `pollardRho`, and `kangaroo` compute point orders and discrete logarithms on the small toy curves (`toy`, `toy2`, `toy3`) only. Because Pohlig-Hellman reduces discrete logarithms to subgroups of prime-factor orders (at most 3 on `toy`, versus 19 on `toy3`) and combines residues with `Modular.crt`, prime group order matters for ECDSA and ECDH.
- `src/Arcanum.Core/Modular.fs`: `modPos`, `xgcd`, `modInverse`, `batchInverse` (Montgomery's trick with a single inversion), `crt`, `jacobi`, `modSqrt`, `reduceSecp256k1`, and `reduceP256` are written in F#. For a non-negative exponent, `modPow` delegates to `BigInteger.ModPow`.
- `src/Arcanum.Core/Primes.fs`: `randomBigInteger` draws uniformly from the inclusive range `[minVal, maxVal]` by bit-masking `RandomNumberGenerator.Fill` draws and rejecting candidates above `maxVal - minVal`. `isPrimeTrialDivision` checks small integers by trial division, `millerRabinWithBases` tests probable primality against caller-supplied bases, and `lucasCertificate` verifies a Lucas primality certificate `(a, primeFactorsOfNMinus1)`.
- `src/Arcanum.Core/Bytes.fs`: `Bytes.constantTimeEquals` XOR-accumulates every byte of two equal-length arrays without an early exit. No constant-time claim is made anywhere in the repository.

The coordinates use F# `BigInteger`; this is not a constant-time field implementation.

## Other exact constructions

- `src/Arcanum.Core/Lattice.fs`: `Lattice.basis` is the 2-by-2 integer basis with rows `(2, 1)` and `(1, 2)`. Its determinant is `3`, and its Gram matrix is `[[5; 4]; [4; 5]]`. The lattice is a matrix of integers. It is not an encryption scheme.
- `src/Arcanum.Core/Shamir.fs`: `Shamir.share` and `reconstruct` implement threshold-2 secret sharing over `ToyField`, while `Shamir.shareWith` and `reconstructMany` evaluate polynomials and Lagrange interpolation at `x = 0` at any threshold over `ToyField`. A share at `x = 0` and two shares with the same `x` are refused. F_17 is not a place to keep a secret.
- `src/Arcanum.Core/FiniteFields.fs` contains `GF256`, the AES field, and `GF128`, the field multiplication described in NIST SP 800-38D. This repository does not implement AES-GCM.

## CLR boundary

- `src/Arcanum.Hashing/SHA256.fs` calls `System.Security.Cryptography.SHA256.HashData`. This repository does not implement the SHA-256 compression function.
- `src/Arcanum.Hashing/HMAC.fs` constructs HMAC in F# and uses `SHA256.hash` for its hash step.
- `src/Arcanum.Hashing/PBKDF2.fs` constructs PBKDF2 in F# and uses `HMAC.hmacSha256` as its pseudorandom function.
- `Rsa.mgf1` uses `SHA256.hash` as its digest.
- `Ecdsa.messageRepresentative` uses `SHA256.hash` to compute the SEC 1 section 4.1.3 big-endian leftmost-bits representative.
- `Rfc6979.generateK` uses `SHA256.hash` for the message digest and `HMAC.hmacSha256` for `HMAC_K`.
- `Bytes.randomBytes` and the random source in `Primes.fs` call `RandomNumberGenerator.Fill`.
- `Modular.modPow` delegates to `BigInteger.ModPow` for a non-negative exponent.

## Symmetric and public-key modules

- `src/Arcanum.Symmetric` contains `Padding.fs`, `Aes.fs`, `Modes.fs`, `ChaCha20.fs`, `Poly1305.fs`, and `ChaCha20Poly1305.fs`.
- `src/Arcanum.Asymmetric` contains `DiffieHellman.fs`, `Rsa.fs`, `Ecdsa.fs`, and `Rfc6979.fs`.
  - `DiffieHellman.rfc3526Group5` is the 1536-bit MODP group from RFC 3526 section 2, with generator 2.
  - `DiffieHellman.computeEcdhSharedSecret` multiplies without point validation; `DiffieHellman.computeEcdhSharedSecretChecked` validates the peer point with `EllipticCurve.validatePublicKey` first and refuses `Infinity` as a shared point per SEC 1 section 3.3.1 step 2.
  - `Rsa.generateKeyPair` requires an even `bits >= 16` and redraws primes until `bitLength (p * q) = bits` exactly. `Rsa.modulusLength` computes the modulus byte length `(bitLength N + 7) / 8`, and `Rsa.encryptOaep` and `Rsa.decryptOaep` implement big-endian I2OSP OAEP encryption and decryption with random seeds (checked as round-trips, not RFC 8017 known-answer vectors).
  - `Ecdsa.messageRepresentative` computes the SEC 1 section 4.1.3 integer representative using `SHA256.hash`. `Ecdsa.signWithNonce` checks that both the private key `d` and nonce `k` lie in `[1, n - 1]`, while `Rfc6979.sign` computes the deterministic RFC 6979 nonce via `Rfc6979.generateK` and `HMAC.hmacSha256` without low-s normalization. `Ecdsa.verify` computes `u1 * G + u2 * Q` via `EllipticCurve.multiScalarMultiply` and refuses `Infinity` and non-invertible `S`. `Ecdsa.recoverPublicKeys` recovers candidate public keys from a message and signature per SEC 1 section 4.1.6.

## Historical mechanisms and demonstrations

- `src/Arcanum.Classical` contains historical mechanisms in `Substitution.fs`, `Polyalphabetic.fs`, `Playfair.fs`, `Enigma.fs`, `Cryptanalysis.fs`, `Hill.fs`, and `Transposition.fs`.
- `src/Arcanum.Cli/Program.fs` is a demonstration runner, including attack demonstrations.

These are historical mechanisms and demonstrations. They do not replace the curve.

## Quickstart

### Prerequisite

- [.NET 10 SDK](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download)

Run the interactive demonstrations:

```bash
dotnet run --project src/Arcanum.Cli/Arcanum.Cli.fsproj
```

Run the tests:

```bash
dotnet test
```

Run the required Release configuration:

```bash
dotnet test --configuration Release
```

## Current test coverage

### Curves

- Complete enumeration of the 18 points on `toy`, out-of-range coordinate rejection in `isOnCurve`, non-singularity discriminant, identity, negation, chord and tangent manual calculations, and the order-3 point (0, 1).
- Group law closure, commutativity on all pairs, and associativity across all 5832 triples of points on `toy`.
- Point order verification via repeated addition, proof that G = (6, 8) generates the 18 points, and agreement between `scalarMultiply` and repeated addition for residues 0 .. 17.
- Negation as a homomorphism on every pair, scalar distributivity over `add` for every residue and pair, and the closed order-3 subgroup {Infinity, (0, 1), (0, 16)} on `toy`.
- `jInvariant` on `toy`, on `toy2`, on `toy3`, and on the singular curve `y^2 = x^3`.
- Complete enumeration of the 16 points on `toy2`, the orders of `G` and (0, 0), the 4-point subgroup of `G`, one chord calculation, closure, commutativity, associativity across all 4096 triples, and agreement between `scalarMultiply` and repeated addition for residues 0 .. 3.
- Complete enumeration of the 19 points on prime-order `toy3`, proof that all 18 non-Infinity points have order 19, manual tangent and chord calculations, closure and commutativity on all 361 pairs, associativity on all 6859 triples, and agreement between `scalarMultiply` and repeated addition for residues 0 .. 18.
- `compress`, `decompress`, and SEC 1 octet encoding/decoding (`Sec1.encodePoint`, `Sec1.decodePoint`) across all points of `toy`, `toy2`, and `toy3`, and on the compressed and uncompressed generators of `secp256k1` and NIST P-256.
- SEC 1 public-key validation (`validatePublicKey`) on `toy3`, `toy`, and non-cyclic `toy2`, invalid-curve parity leak and refusal via `computeEcdhSharedSecretChecked` on `toy`, small-subgroup and full-order `Infinity` shared-point refusals in `computeEcdhSharedSecretChecked`, small-subgroup scalar leak (`d mod 3`) via `Point(0, 1)` on composite-order `toy`, and ECDH agreement across all 324 private-key pairs on prime-order `toy3`.
- Point-order histograms (`DiscreteLog.order`) on `toy`, `toy2`, and `toy3`, 2-torsion points, and the explicit `Z/4 x Z/4` bijection `a * G + b * Q` on `toy2`.
- Jacobian representation (`ofAffine`, `toAffine`), projective scaling equivalence, and agreement of `Jacobian.double`, `Jacobian.add`, `Jacobian.addMixed`, `Jacobian.scalarMultiply`, `Jacobian.doubleAMinus3` (on `a = -3` NIST P-256), and `Jacobian.batchToAffine` with the affine group law across `toy`, `toy2`, `toy3`, `secp256k1`, and NIST P-256.
- Agreement of `scalarMultiplyLadder`, `naf`, `scalarMultiplyNaf`, `wnaf`, `scalarMultiplyWnaf`, and `multiScalarMultiply` (Shamir's trick) with `scalarMultiply` and `add` across `toy`, `toy2`, `toy3`, `secp256k1`, and NIST P-256.
- Toy-curve discrete logarithms via `DiscreteLog.bruteForce`, `babyStepGiantStep`, `pohligHellman`, `pollardRho`, and `kangaroo` on `toy` and `toy3`.
- Legendre-symbol point counting (`countPoints`), Frobenius traces (`0`, `2`, `-1`), integer Hasse bound checks (`t^2 <= 4p`), and quadratic twists (`twistCoefficients`) on `toy`, `toy2`, and `toy3`.
- Generator validation on `secp256k1` and NIST P-256, unreduced double-and-add order checks (`N * G = Infinity` and `(N - 1) * G = -G`), and the `secp256k1` cube-root endomorphism (`beta` mod `p`, `lambda` mod `N`) checked on `G` and `2G` without discrete logarithms or GLV scalar decomposition.
- ECDH shared point equality on `toy`, SEC 1 `messageRepresentative` on NIST P-256 and `toy3`, ECDSA `signWithNonce` (including private-key range `[1, n - 1]` and the full 18-nonce sweep on `toy3`), `verify` (via `multiScalarMultiply`), `recoverPublicKeys` (SEC 1 section 4.1.6 on `toy3` and RFC 6979 NIST P-256 vectors), signature malleability `(r, N - s)` without normalization, and closed-form recovery of the private scalar and nonce from repeated or affine-related (`k2 = a * k1 + b`) nonces on `toy3`.

### Arithmetic and other constructions

- Exact field residues modulo 17, non-zero multiplicative inverses, and Fermat's Little Theorem.
- `xgcd` Bezout identities, `batchInverse` (Montgomery's trick), `crt`, `jacobi`, `modSqrt`, `reduceSecp256k1`, and `reduceP256`.
- Inclusive `Primes.randomBigInteger`, trial-division primality on small primes and composites, `millerRabinWithBases` (including Carmichael number `561`, strong pseudoprime `3215031751`, and probable-prime checks on `secp256k1` and NIST P-256 parameters), and `lucasCertificate` for `17` and `19`.
- `Bytes.constantTimeEquals` byte comparison checks.
- GF(2^8) arithmetic and AES S-box entries, and structural checks of GF(2^128) multiplication. The GF(2^128) checks are not a GHASH vector.
- The lattice determinant and Gram matrix.
- Shamir threshold-2 (`share`, `reconstruct`) and arbitrary-threshold (`shareWith`, `reconstructMany`) secret sharing over `ToyField`, including 2-share equivocation across all 17 secrets for a degree-2 polynomial.
- Textbook RSA on a small key, decryption by the Chinese Remainder Theorem, exact modulus bit length in `Rsa.generateKeyPair`, toy Diffie-Hellman modulo 23, the shape of the RFC 3526 group 5 record, one MGF1 counter block, and `Rsa.modulusLength`.

### Cited checks

Each of these is a published example, not a CAVP campaign:

- AES-128 key expansion and single-block encryption/decryption against FIPS 197 Appendices A.1, B, and C.1. ECB on Appendix C.1 carries the same block, followed by a PKCS#7 padding block that is not a second vector.
- AES-128 in ECB, CBC, and CTR modes against NIST SP 800-38A sections F.1.1, F.2.1, F.5.1, and F.5.2.
- The ChaCha20 block function and stream cipher against RFC 8439 sections 2.3.2 and 2.4.2 and Appendix A.2 (#2 and #3).
- Poly1305 MAC and one-time key generation against RFC 8439 sections 2.5.2 and 2.6.2 and Appendix A.3 (#1, #4, and #5 through #11).
- ChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD encryption, decryption, and tag/AAD tamper rejection against RFC 8439 section 2.8.2 and Appendix A.5.
- HMAC-SHA256 against RFC 4231 test cases 1 through 7.
- The one-iteration PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 answer for password `password` and salt `salt`, and the two RFC 7914 section 11 64-byte PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 vectors (`c = 1` and `c = 80000`).
- The double of the SEC 2 `secp256k1` generator.
- RFC 6979 section A.1.2 (`SHA256("sample")`) and section A.2.5 deterministic ECDSA on NIST P-256 with SHA-256 (octet conversions, public key, deterministic nonces `k`, `(r, s)` signatures for `"sample"` and `"test"`, and SEC 1 public-key recovery).
- RFC 5903 section 8.1 ECDH on NIST P-256 (initiator and responder public points and shared point/secret `girx`).
- The results of the CLR SHA-256 library call for `"abc"` and the empty message against their published digests. These checks do not demonstrate an in-repository SHA-256 compression function.

### Round trips and demonstrations

- Caesar, Atbash, affine, Vigenere, Playfair, Hill (`Hill.encrypt`, `Hill.decrypt`), and rail fence (`Transposition.railFenceEncrypt`, `Transposition.railFenceDecrypt`) hand checks, index of coincidence fractions, and a Caesar break by chi-squared.
- Enigma reciprocity.
- AES-CBC, PKCS#7, and ChaCha20-Poly1305 round trips. The ChaCha20-Poly1305 fact also rejects one tampered ciphertext; it is not an RFC 8439 vector file. Poly1305 clamp and empty-message checks are structural.
- RSA-OAEP (`encryptOaep` and `decryptOaep`) round trips and ciphertext/label tampering rejections; these use random seeds and are not RFC 8017 known-answer vectors.
- Agreement between two locally generated ECDH parties; this is not a published shared-secret vector.
- ECDSA sign-then-verify and rejection of a modified message; this is not a published signature vector.

Round-trip checks are not NIST CAVP vectors.
